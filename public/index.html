Property Legal Verification & FAQ

Every listing undergoes 100% field, document, and legal scrutiny by Mr. K. Kulasekaran.

✓ 1. How does Tamizh Real Estate verify 30-Year Encumbrance Certificates (EC)? ▼

We extract the certified 30-year online and offline Encumbrance Certificate from the Sub-Registrar Office (Guduvanchery / Chengalpattu). This ensures zero prior bank mortgages, no court disputes, and unbroken ancestral title continuity.

✓ 2. How is Immediate Patta Transfer guaranteed after registration? ▼

All our approved layout plots have individual survey subdivision numbers (உட்பிரிவு பட்டா). Immediately after your sale deed registration, our team coordinates directly with the local Taluk Office for name transfer into your name.

✓ 3. Can I get up to 80% bank loan for layout plots? ▼

Yes! Because our layout promotion projects carry DTCP and Panchayat sanctions, nationalized and leading private banks (State Bank of India, HDFC, LIC Housing Finance, and Canara Bank) approve up to 75%–80% loan amounts with swift document processing.
